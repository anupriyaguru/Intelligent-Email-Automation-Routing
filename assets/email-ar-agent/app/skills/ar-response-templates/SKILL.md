# AR Response Templates

## Account Statement Response

"Dear [BP_NAME], please find attached your account statement for [PERIOD]. Your current outstanding balance is [AMOUNT]. Please review and contact us if you have any questions. Your reference number is [CASE_ID]."

## Open Items Response

"Dear [BP_NAME], as of [DATE], the following invoices remain outstanding: [OPEN_ITEMS_LIST]. Please remit payment at your earliest convenience. Your reference number is [CASE_ID]."

## Dispute Acknowledgment

"Dear [BP_NAME], we have received your dispute for invoice [INVOICE_NUMBER] for [AMOUNT]. A dispute case has been created with reference [DISPUTE_ID]. Our team will review and respond within [SLA_DAYS] business days. Your reference number is [CASE_ID]."
