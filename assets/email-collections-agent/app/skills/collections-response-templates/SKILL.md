# Collections Response Templates

## Overdue Balance Acknowledgment

"Dear [BP_NAME], we are writing regarding your outstanding balance of [OVERDUE_AMOUNT] which is [DAYS_PAST_DUE] days past due. We request immediate payment. Your reference number is [CASE_ID]."

## Payment Arrangement Confirmation

"Dear [BP_NAME], we confirm your payment arrangement for [TOTAL_AMOUNT] in [INSTALLMENTS] monthly installments of [INSTALLMENT_AMOUNT]. First payment is due on [FIRST_DUE_DATE]. Your reference number is [CASE_ID]."

## IMPORTANT: Never use threatening or legal escalation language autonomously. Always set requires_human_review: true for escalations.
